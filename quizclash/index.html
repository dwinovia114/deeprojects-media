How To Play

1. Team Blue stands on the LEFT. Team Yellow stands on the RIGHT.

2. Point your index finger to aim at the falling answer options.

3. Hold to Select: Keep your finger steady on the correct answer for 1.2 seconds to lock and select it safely!

4. Speed Bonus: Answer correctly and quickly to earn extra points!

5. Wrong answer locks your team out of the round, giving the other team a chance to answer.
